## Dadyoom learning pyramid (2026-10-19)
- Keep Arabic learning packs original, linguistically reviewed, and traceable to real lesson objectives; do not claim formal curriculum alignment without evidence.
- Six stages: discover -> understand -> apply -> analyze -> evaluate -> create. Creative work is self-reviewed and requires teacher approval before mastery can be asserted.
- Teacher editor exports a local draft only; it must not automatically publish a student-facing official lesson or collect learner free-writing on the server.
- Reuse the core Dadyoom app, keep a standalone working lab route, mobile-ready CSS layers and keyboard accessible actions, and preserve the official 22-country curriculum-first priority.
